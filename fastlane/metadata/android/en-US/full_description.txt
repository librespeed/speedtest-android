LibreSpeed measures your internet connection speed against community-run LibreSpeed servers or your own self-hosted one.

Features:

- Download, upload, ping, jitter and approximate packet loss
- IPv4 and IPv6 support
- Server selection with favorites and custom self-hosted servers
- Test history with sharing
- Dark and light theme, Material 3 design
- No ads, no tracking; telemetry is strictly opt-in
